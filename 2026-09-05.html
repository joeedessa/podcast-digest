THESIS: Persistent fiscal deficits, currency debasement, and the weaponization of the dollar are rendering traditional government bonds ineffective as portfolio diversifiers. KEY TENSION: A sustained period of non-inflationary growth combined with a credible return to fiscal discipline would restore faith in fiat currencies and government bonds, challenging the necessity of holding non-yielding real assets.
