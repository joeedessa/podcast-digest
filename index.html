TL;DR (read this first)

A starkly bearish consensus is forming, with key voices calling for a significant market downturn. Mike McGlone sees an imminent global deflationary recession triggered by an overvalued US stock market, making long-duration Treasuries the primary safe haven. Chance Finucane targets 2027 for a 30%+ equity decline driven by slowing growth and inflation, preferring the safety of short-duration bonds. Against this macro risk-off backdrop, the AI theme is evolving from a simple hardware trade into a more complex landscape of second-order effects and new bottlenecks, where specialists like Citrini see selective opportunities.

  • CENTRAL TENSION: A macro-driven imperative to de-risk all assets versus a micro-driven search for selective opportunities within the AI disruption.
  • TOP THEME: The Evolving AI Trade — Focus is shifting from frothy hardware to second-order effects and real-world application bottlenecks.
  • DIVERGENT: US Treasuries — LONG long-duration as the best alpha trade (McGlone) vs. LONG short-duration for safety in a volatile yield environment (Finucane).
  • HIGH-CONVICTION TRADE: Reduce/Avoid AI Stocks, supported by Chance Finucane and Citrini, who both see the sector as overvalued and increasingly risky.
  • CALENDAR: US Midterm Elections, cited as a potential catalyst for a market shift.

DOMINANT THEMES

The Evolving AI Trade (3 shows)

THESIS: The initial, monolithic AI hardware boom is maturing and facing risks from reflexive financing and stretched valuations. This is forcing investors to shift focus to second-order effects, identifying new bottlenecks in real-world applications and unique hedges against AI's disruptive economic impact.

CAUTIOUS ON HARDWARE (Chance Finucane, Citrini) The consensus is that the easy money in the AI infrastructure trade has been made. Chance Finucane — Adam Taggart | Thoughtful Money® sees AI beneficiary stocks as too highly priced with 30-50% downside risk. Citrini — The Monetary Matters Network notes that the financing of the AI buildout has shifted from hyperscaler cash flow to more speculative equity and debt, creating a reflexive loop that is vulnerable to shifts in sentiment. He has been reducing his broad AI hardware exposure since March.

FOCUS ON APPLICATION & SECOND-ORDER EFFECTS (Citrini, Pascal Hügli) With the hardware trade now more complex, capital is seeking opportunities further down the value chain. Citrini — The Monetary Matters Network is now "long experiments," identifying bottlenecks in the application of AI, particularly in drug discovery where physical lab work (like that done by CRL) cannot be accelerated. This creates a durable investment thesis. Separately, Pascal Hügli — Less Noise More Signal reframes Bitcoin as a necessary asset in the age of AI, arguing its digital scarcity is the only true moat against the hyper-competition AI will unleash, making it the "purest AI trade."

KEY TENSION: Resolution depends on whether AI-driven Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) can accelerate to justify the massive infrastructure spending, or if a "capability-ARR gap" emerges, triggering a downturn in the AI hardware sector.

The Coming Deflationary Downturn (2 shows)

THESIS: A significant market correction and economic downturn are widely anticipated, driven by overvaluation and the lagged effects of monetary tightening. The key debate is not if, but when, and which assets will provide the best protection.

DEFLATIONARY RECESSION CAMP (Mike McGlone) Mike McGlone — Wealth Building Blueprint – Vladyslav Grabarskyy argues that the US stock market is the final, overvalued domino to fall, which will trigger a "global deflationary recession." He sees this as a normal reversion after the massive COVID-era stimulus. In this scenario, long-duration US Treasuries are the premier asset, offering both safety and alpha as yields collapse.

2027 SLOWDOWN CAMP (Chance Finucane) Chance Finucane — Adam Taggart | Thoughtful Money® is specifically concerned about 2027, predicting a stock market decline of 30% or more. His catalyst is the combination of slowing economic growth and slowing inflation—historically the worst environment for stocks. His preferred havens are short-duration Treasuries for their safe yield and reasonably-valued, non-AI equities in sectors like energy and healthcare.

KEY TENSION: The timing and nature of the downturn will determine whether long-duration bonds (McGlone's recession trade) or short-duration bonds and value stocks (Finucane's slowdown trade) offer superior risk-adjusted returns.

SPOTLIGHT: Bitcoin Isn't an Anti-Government Trade, It's an AI Trade

Host: Pascal Hügli Guest: Solo episode.

  • Pascal Hügli argues that Bitcoin's core utility is not as an ideological tool against government debasement, but as a practical, digitally scarce store of value essential for the AI-driven economy.
  • He posits that AI will commoditize nearly everything and erode traditional business moats, including those of the "Mag 7," by enabling unlimited competition.
  • In this environment, Bitcoin's provable and finite scarcity provides a unique and durable moat that cannot be outcompeted, making it the "purest AI trade."
  • Hügli asserts that Bitcoin has already won the "digital store of value" race, citing widespread adoption and institutional interest.
  • He challenges the "liquidity equals bullish Bitcoin" narrative, suggesting that as Bitcoin gains fundamental use cases, its value will be driven more by the velocity of money and usage, not just central bank balance sheets.
