SPOTLIGHT: Global Upheaval in Energy Markets: Fred Hutchison

Host: David Greely Guest: Fred Hutchison, President and CEO, LNG Allies

  • Fred Hutchison detailed the rise of the US to become the world's leading LNG exporter, driven by the shale revolution and geopolitical demand for non-Russian gas, particularly from Europe.

SPOTLIGHT: The Case for Public Markets (Cromwell Coulson)

Host: Jack Farley Guest: Cromwell Coulson, CEO, OTC Markets Group

  • Cromwell Coulson highlighted upcoming SEC rule changes that he believes will transform capital access for smaller public companies, including those on the OTCQX and OTCQB markets.
  • The new rules will expand At-The-Market (ATM) offering programs to these companies and preempt state-level "blue sky" rules for SEC-registered documents, streamlining the capital-raising process.
  • Coulson argued that the regulatory system is biased against public companies, and these changes will help make public markets more competitive with private equity and venture capital.
