THESIS: The unexpected Democratic sweep ("Blue Nami") and widespread local opposition to data centers have shattered the "growth at any cost" narrative for the AI buildout, triggering a violent repricing in semiconductors and related tech stocks. The market is now forced to confront the reality that the AI boom is subject to political, regulatory, and economic constraints.

Howard Mason — RenMac argues that demand for AI compute is "inelastic to price" because the penalty for being short compute is "existential." KEY TENSION: Whether the sell-off is a temporary repricing of political risk within a continuing (but more expensive) buildout, or the beginning of a full-blown bust that drags down the broader economy and forces a Fed pivot.
