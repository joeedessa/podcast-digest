SPOTLIGHT: The Awesome Portfolio

Host: Tony Greer, Jared Dillian Guest: Jared Dillian

  • Thesis: Jared Dillian — The Macro Dirt Podcast advocates for his "Awesome Portfolio," a 5x20% allocation to stocks, bonds, gold, real estate, and cash, as a superior alternative to the 60/40 portfolio for the average investor.
  • Core Argument: While the portfolio's 9% historical return is slightly lower than the 60/40's 9.44%, it drastically cuts volatility and drawdowns (e.g., -9% in 2008 vs. -57% for the S&P 500), making it a powerful "financial psychology management tool."
  • Asset Rationale: The portfolio diversifies between financial assets (stocks, bonds) and hard assets (gold, real estate, cash), providing better protection in environments where stocks and bonds fall together. Gold is chosen over a broad commodity index for its low cost of carry.
  • Implementation: The real estate portion is typically the equity in one's primary residence. Dillian advises against tactical overweighting or underweighting by non-experts, recommending sticking to the 20% allocations.
  • Market Context: Tony Greer — The Macro Dirt Podcast notes that Morgan Stanley's 2025 shift to a "60/20/20" allocation (stocks/bonds/gold) has opened the "Overton window" for such alternative portfolio constructions.
