WEEK IN REVIEW

The AI momentum trade cracked this week, forcing a violent rotation as the market shifted from celebrating a technological super-cycle to scrutinizing its immense cost. This created a stark tension between the market's near-term financial reckoning and the narrative of long-term technological transformation.
