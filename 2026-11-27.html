SPOTLIGHT: The Essential Steps To Retire Well - Are You Following Them?

Host: Adam Taggart Guest: Julia Lembcke — URS Advisory

  • This episode provides a detailed guide to retirement planning, focusing on tax diversification, healthcare optimization, and estate planning for different age brackets (55-75+).
  • A key strategy is to diversify savings across pre-tax (401k/IRA), Roth, and after-tax brokerage accounts to create flexibility and control taxable income in retirement.
  • For those retiring before 65, navigating healthcare is critical. Options include ACA plans, COBRA, and spousal plans, with a strong recommendation to manage income to qualify for premium subsidies.
  • Lembcke strongly prefers Medicare Supplement plans over Medicare Advantage for their comprehensive coverage and freedom of provider choice, but warns the decision at age 65 is critical due to later underwriting restrictions.
  • Comprehensive estate planning (wills, trusts, power of attorney) is emphasized as essential to avoid family conflict and ensure one's wishes are honored.

SPOTLIGHT: The AI Hacking Arms Race Has Begun, Can Bitcoin Keep Up?

Host: Pascal Hügli Guest: Jameson Lopp — Casa

  • The pace of discovering software vulnerabilities has dramatically accelerated due to advanced AI models, particularly open-weight Chinese models with fewer guardrails, forcing a shift to real-time security patching.
  • This new environment poses a significant risk to any internet-facing software, especially "hot wallets" and Layer 2 protocols in the crypto space, which are now under constant threat.
  • Lopp remains optimistic that the Bitcoin base layer protocol itself is secure and will not be broken, as vulnerabilities are typically found in adjacent software implementations.
  • For significant Bitcoin holdings, a simple hardware wallet is no longer sufficient. The "ultimate tier" of security is a distributed, multi-vendor, multi-key, multi-signature setup to eliminate single points of failure.
  • The risk to Bitcoin from quantum computing is considered a distant, "edge case" issue, not a near-term threat.
