Morning Briefing Coppers Squeeze Continues to Tighten

Host: Trevor Hall · Solo episode · Listen at source ↗

Overview

Trevor Hall reports on a strong morning for precious metals, with gold, silver, and platinum all seeing significant gains. Copper is also modestly higher, nearing record territory due to a supply squeeze exacerbated by an outage at the Gresik smelter in Indonesia. All eyes are on the July CPI inflation report, which is expected to be a key determinant for market direction and the Federal Reserve's September rate cut decision.

Key discussion points

  • Precious metals performance: Trevor Hall notes that gold is up 1% to $4,413/ounce, silver is up 2.5% to $66.31, and platinum is the outperformer, up 2.7% to $1,082.
  • Copper supply squeeze: Trevor Hall highlights that copper is near record territory, up three quarters to $6.64/pound, due to an outage at the Gresik smelter in Indonesia, which processes ore from the Grassberg mine. London benchmark copper has held above $14,000/ton, and Comex contracts for 2007 delivery are trading above $7/pound.
  • July CPI inflation report: Trevor Hall states that economists are forecasting a 0.2% increase in headline consumer prices, which would bring the annual rate down to 3.4%. Core inflation is estimated at 0.1% monthly and 2.5% annually, prints that would be consistent with progress towards the Fed's 2% target and could lock in a September rate cut. An upside surprise would complicate this picture.
  • Integra Resources Q2 2026 results: Trevor Hall reports Integra Resources produced 16,379 ounces of gold, a 30% increase quarter-over-quarter, with revenue of $70.88 million from selling 15,794 ounces at an average realized price of $4,426 per ounce. The Delamar project entered federal NEPA permitting in May.
  • Elemental Royalty Q2 performance: Trevor Hall notes Elemental Royalty reported record quarterly gold equivalent ounces sold (5,248 GEOs, up 65% YoY) and record operating cash flow ($15.5 million), generating $23.8 million in revenue. The company remains on track to deliver 17,000 to 21,000 GEOs for the year.
