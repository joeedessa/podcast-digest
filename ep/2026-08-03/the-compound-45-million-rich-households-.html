Overview

This episode of The Compound, hosted by Michael Batnick, discusses the current market environment, highlighting a significant divergence between semiconductor stocks and the broader market. Batnick observes "late cycle behavior" but notes the market's resilience, even as "leadership stocks" are "absolutely hammered." The episode also delves into the underperformance of IPOs, the widespread bearish sentiment on bonds despite higher yields, and the increasing participation of individual investors in private markets.
