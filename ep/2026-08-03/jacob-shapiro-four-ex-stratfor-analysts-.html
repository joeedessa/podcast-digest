Overview

This episode features a reunion of four ex-Stratfor analysts, including Peter, discussing the future of geopolitics and the potential "end of the world" over the next 10 years. Peter argues that deglobalization and demographics are the primary drivers of systemic vulnerability, leading to a potential collapse of global trade and industrial systems. The discussion explores various scenarios, from a "Mad Max" energy crisis to the dissolution of nation-states and the rise of new military technologies, with a focus on the implications for global stability and economic systems.
