Rick Rule Preparing for a Stock Market Crash Why Gold Demand Could 4x

Host: Vladyslav Grabarskyy · Guest: Rick Rule, President and CEO of Rule Investment Media · Listen at source ↗

Overview

Rick Rule discusses the long-term outlook for gold, precious metals, and energy, emphasizing the potential for a liquidity-driven market crash similar to 2008. He highlights the importance of maintaining liquidity to capitalize on such events and advocates for a contrarian, value-focused approach to investing, particularly in under-loved sectors like gold and uranium. Rule also stresses the significance of patience and thorough research in speculative markets.

Key discussion points

  • Rick Rule is "extremely confident in the long-term outlook for gold" over the next 10 years due to declining US dollar purchasing power from debt and deficits, despite potential near-term softness in 2026 due to higher US interest rates.
  • Rule believes precious metals bull markets are led by gold, with silver typically outperforming later as generalist investors enter the market due to its lower unit price.
  • Rule sees platinum as attractive due to its out-of-mainstream status, critical role in autocatalysts (contrary to the belief that internal combustion engines are obsolete), and potential supply disruptions from major producers like South Africa, Zimbabwe, and Russia.
  • Rule advises that investors in mining stocks should choose between market beta (e.g., Franco-Nevada, Wheaton Precious, Agnico) for less work and volatility, or actively seek takeover targets among smaller companies if willing to do the extensive research.
  • Rule notes that the oil industry's underinvestment of "over a billion U.S. dollars a day" will lead to structural shortages by 2029-2030, making it a good time to be an oil investor, though governments will likely "steal a greater and greater and greater amount of free cash flow" through taxes.
  • Rule states that Americans are "stupidly uninvested in gold and silver," with precious metals comprising less than 0.5% of total savings and assets, down from a four-decade mean of 2%. A reversion to the mean would imply a "fourfold increase in demand."
  • Rule believes the "easy money has been made" in uranium since prices were $20/pound, but "the sure money is ahead of us," emphasizing that the market trend will take "three years or four years or five years to play out."
  • Rule highlights the importance of maintaining liquidity, viewing the 3.3% negative yield on the US 10-year Treasury (given 8% compounded dollar decline) as an "option premium" for capitalizing on a potential "liquidity-driven crash" similar to 2008, which led to his "best single investment year" in 2009.

Market & macro takeaways

  • Rick Rule believes the US dollar's purchasing power will decline substantially over the next 10 years due to debt and deficits, supporting a long-term bullish view on gold.
  • Rule suggests that higher US interest rates could keep gold soft or cause it to decline through the end of 2026.
  • Rule identifies platinum as an attractive, under-discussed asset with strong fundamentals, including its role in autocatalysts and potential supply disruptions from key producing nations.
  • Rule anticipates structural oil shortages by 2029-2030 due to sustained underinvestment in the industry, which will lead to higher real oil prices, but also increased government taxation ("theft") of free cash flow.
  • Rule notes that the US market is significantly underinvested in gold and silver relative to historical averages, suggesting a potential "fourfold increase in demand" if market share reverts to the mean.
  • Rule views the current negative real yield on US Treasuries as an "option premium" for maintaining liquidity, which could provide significant opportunities during a liquidity-driven market crash.
  • Rule emphasizes Pareto's Law in investing, suggesting that a small percentage of management teams generate the majority of profits, and investors should concentrate on these "high quality people."
