ALERT 2027 Will Be A Disaster For Both Stocks Bonds Michael Pento

Host: Adam Taggart · Guest: Michael Pento, President and Founder, Pento Portfolio Strategies · Listen at source ↗

Overview

Michael Pento discusses his 12-point model, which has shifted from net long to neutral, signaling a "very difficult year" for both stock and bond markets in 2027. He attributes this shift to the Federal Reserve's actions, the fracturing bond market, and deteriorating market breadth. Pento believes the Fed will eventually be forced to intervene but only after significant market pain, leading to a period of "hyper stagflation."

Key discussion points

  • Michael Pento's model has shifted to neutral: Pento, who was net long equities since 2023, has now neutralized his portfolio. He states, "I am not net short yet. I've neutralized the portfolio." This is the first such shift since December 2021, when he became neutral and then bearish.
  • Three reasons for the shift: Pento identifies three primary factors: 1) The Fed is pricking the equity bubble by raising the Fed funds rate, which primarily impacts the debt-fueled AI investment bubble. 2) The bond market is fracturing due to persistent inflation, national insolvency (debt-to-revenue ratio of 720%), foreign creditors shunning treasuries, the Bank of Japan selling treasuries, and global anchors like German bunds and JGBs offering competitive yields. 3) Deteriorating market breadth, with over 70% of the S&P 500 in a correction, 60% down over 20%, and 40% down over 30%.
  • Fed's reluctant intervention: Pento believes the Fed, under Kevin Warsh, will be reluctant to monetize debt and cut rates with "alacrity" as in the past. He expects them to "let this thing really bite first" before being "dragged into the fight" when banks close and money markets freeze.
  • 2027 outlook: Pento predicts 2027 will be a "disaster" for stocks and bonds, potentially more violent than 2022. He also expects the credit bubble to pop, impacting the real estate market, with home prices crashing and the stock market (currently 235% of GDP) having "a long way to go down."
  • Potential delays to the outlook: Pento identifies two "escape patches": a "dramatic and quick resolution to the war in Iran" leading to lower energy prices, and a "massive productivity boom from this all this capex spending in AI." He views these as "a big if."
  • Bond market strategy: Pento is buying one-to-three-year treasuries, believing the two-year note at 4.8% has already priced in three potential rate hikes. He expects a "Pavlovian response" into long-term bonds during a recession but believes it will be "muted at best" and short-lived due to exploding deficits (potentially $6 trillion) and the Fed's reluctance to monetize.
  • Sector 4 and future shifts: Pento's model is currently in Sector 4 (rising inflation and growth on a second derivative basis), which is typically a long position. However, he is ignoring the normal playbook due to the bond market's fracturing, expecting a shift to Sector 2 and then Sector 1 (deflation/all hell breaks loose).
  • Four Horsemen portfolio: In Sector 1, Pento's "Four Horsemen" portfolio consists of cash, T-bills, US dollar investments, a little bit of gold, and shorts. He has already added CTAs and two short ETFs to his current neutral portfolio.
