Host: Adam Taggart · Guest: Michael Pento, Founder and CEO of Pento Portfolio Strategies

Overview

Michael Pento discusses the current economic environment, characterized by compressed and volatile market cycles driven by geopolitical events, particularly the conflict with Iran. He believes the economy is heading towards a deflationary depression, followed by hyper-stagflation, due to excessive debt and asset bubbles in equities, credit, and real estate. Pento outlines his portfolio strategy, which currently maintains a neutral stance in "sector 3" of his model, but anticipates a shift towards deflationary and then hyper-stagflationary environments.

Key discussion points

  • Michael Pento's proprietary 20-point model has five stages, and the portfolio is currently in "sector 3," a stasis phase, due to compressed and volatile market cycles since February 28th.
  • Pento believes the current "sojourn into chaos" will end in disinflation, then deflation, and ultimately a depression, not a recession, which he refers to as "sector one" in his model.
  • The US Federal Reserve, under new Chair Kevin Worsh, faces a challenge in controlling inflation, with the balance sheet having grown by $38 billion since May 22nd, despite Worsh's stated commitment to reducing inflation to 2%.
  • Pento highlights the immense credit bubble, including $1.6 trillion in private credit, $1.4 trillion in collateralized loan obligations, and $1.5 trillion in junk bonds, alongside a national debt of $40 trillion (123% of GDP).
  • Morgan Stanley estimates over $570 billion in AI-related debt will be raised in 2026, adding to the debt burden and crowding out other borrowing.
  • The bond market is revolting, with 30-year bond yields at their highest since 2007, driven by the crowding out effect of AI and national debt, and exacerbated by the Bank of Japan selling treasuries to support the yen.
  • Pento expects a deflationary depression to lead to a 30% drop in the real estate market and a "north of 50%" fall in the stock market to return to historical valuation metrics.
  • Following the deflationary depression, Pento anticipates a massive government response, including automatic stabilizers and potential new programs, leading to deficits of $6 trillion, which the Fed will monetize, resulting in "hyper stagflation like we have never seen before."
  • Pento believes there will be another "lost decade" for stock investing, citing historical periods of low average annual returns following the Great Depression, the stagflationary era, and the dot-com bubble.
  • The AI mania is showing signs of cooling, with the semiconductor sector (SMH) appearing to have peaked and the South Korean stock market experiencing a significant correction.
  • The plummeting cost of AI compute, as evidenced by new Chinese models, poses a threat to the profit models of major hyperscalers and AI players, potentially leading to slashed profit forecasts.
  • Pento argues that the borrowing costs for AI-related debt will rise significantly, putting pressure on cash flow and cost flow analysis for issuing this debt, especially for long-duration bonds.
  • The economy is already starting to slow significantly in the second half of 2026, with the household survey showing a loss of 57,000 jobs in June and 1.7 million jobs year-to-date.
  • Pento leans towards disinflation heading towards deflation and recession in the near term, but acknowledges that an escalation of the conflict in Iraq could change this outlook.
