Overview

Tony Greer observes a "big breakout day" in the stock market, driven by the removal of uncertainties such as the Iran war's diminishing market impact, a significant dollar-yen intervention, and Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh's "play the ball" approach to inflation data. He highlights two-sigma rallies in internet stocks, airlines, and software, and a bullish move in the MAG7, suggesting a "green light go" for the market despite underlying fragilities in the bond market and persistent inflation concerns. Greer emphasizes trading the market in front of you, adapting to rolling momentum moves across different sectors.
