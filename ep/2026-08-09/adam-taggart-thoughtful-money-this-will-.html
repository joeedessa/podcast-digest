This Will End In A Deflationary Depression Michael Pento

Host: Adam Taggart · Guest: Michael Pento, Founder and CEO of Pento Portfolio Strategies · Listen at source ↗

Overview

Michael Pento believes the current economic environment, characterized by compressed and volatile cycles driven by geopolitical events and fiscal/monetary policy, will eventually lead to a deflationary depression. He argues that unprecedented asset bubbles in equities, credit, and real estate, combined with unsustainable national debt and the Federal Reserve's policy challenges, set the stage for a significant market correction and a "lost decade" for investors. Pento emphasizes the need for active management to navigate these turbulent times, which he expects to be followed by a period of hyperstagflation due to aggressive government intervention.

Key discussion points

  • Michael Pento's proprietary 20-point model, which usually sees cycles lasting months and years, has experienced compressed cycles of weeks and days since February 28th, due to geopolitical tensions with Iran.
  • Pento believes the current situation will eventually lead to a "deflationary depression," which he defines as Sector One in his model, following a period of disinflation.
  • Pento attributes the recent spike in bond yields to the crowding out effect from AI-related debt and national debt, exacerbated by the Bank of Japan selling treasuries to support the yen.
  • Pento argues that the Federal Reserve, under Kevin Warsh, will attempt to shrink its balance sheet, which he expects will burst the credit bubble and lead to a market crash, forcing the Fed to monetize debt again, resulting in hyperstagflation.
  • Pento highlights the "triumvirate of bubbles" in equities, credit, and real estate, noting that each is at record proportions and existing concurrently, making the current market environment uniquely dangerous.
  • Pento expects significant corrections in asset prices, with real estate needing a 25-30% drop to return to historical home price-to-income ratios, and the stock market needing "something north of 50%" to return to historical valuation metrics.
  • Pento believes the "K-shaped economy" will see further devastation for the lower 80% of the population during a deflationary depression and subsequent hyperstagflation, as inflation disproportionately impacts their purchasing power.
  • Pento sees signs of cooling in the AI mania, citing the South Korean stock market's 40% drop in a handful of trading days and plummeting compute costs for AI models, which threaten the profit models of major AI players.
  • Pento warns of a "lost decade" for stock investing, citing historical precedents where average annual returns were near zero for extended periods following major economic crises, and expects a return to active management.
  • Pento notes that the economy is already showing signs of slowing, with the household survey indicating a loss of 507,000 jobs in June and 1.7 million jobs year-to-date in 2026, despite the establishment survey showing otherwise.
  • Pento's current portfolio is positioned in Sector Three (stasis), holding equal-weight S&P 500, some gold, international equities, and hedges, due to the whipsawing market conditions.
  • Pento leans towards a disinflationary path heading towards deflation and recession in the near term, but acknowledges that an escalation in the Middle East conflict could shift the outlook towards inflation.
