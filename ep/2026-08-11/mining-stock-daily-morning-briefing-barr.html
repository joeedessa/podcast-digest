Morning Briefing Barrick and Newmont Come to Terms with Nevada Gold Mines JV

Host: Trevor Hall · Solo episode · Listen at source ↗

Overview

Trevor Hall reports on mixed metals performance, with gold down, silver up, and copper outperforming. He highlights the Pentagon's request to accelerate weapons production due to conflict-exacerbated shortages and President Trump's meeting with mining executives to promote US critical minerals investments. The episode also covers a significant amendment to the Nevada Gold Mines joint venture between Barrick Mining and Newmont, resolving disputes and facilitating Barrick's proposed North American gold assets IPO.

Key discussion points

  • Pentagon's request to accelerate weapons production: Trevor Hall notes that the Pentagon's number two official has directly asked CEOs of major US defense companies to speed up production and delivery of key weapons systems, citing "mounting concerns about shortages and capability gaps that have been exacerbated by the ongoing conflict with Iran." This signals the administration views current stockpile levels as "dangerously insufficient."
  • US critical minerals investments: Trevor Hall reports that President Trump convened a meeting with top mining executives, including leadership from Rio Tinto, BHP, and Freeport-McMoRan, to promote over $3 billion in US critical minerals investments. These investments aim to reduce American dependence on China-dominated supply chains, including a $1.4 billion Pentagon investment in a domestic battery facility, $400 million for Scandium production, and over $1 billion in export-import bank refinancing for Ivanhoe Electric's Santa Cruz project in Arizona.
  • Barrick and Newmont Nevada Gold Mines JV amendment: Trevor Hall details a "landmark agreement" between Barrick Mining and Newmont to amend their Nevada Gold Mines joint venture. Newmont will pay Barrick "just about $2 billion" to include previously excluded properties like Barrick's Four Mile deposit and Newmont's Fiberline and Mike developments into the JV, resolving all outstanding disputes. The deal also includes Newmont's consent to Barrick's proposed IPO of its North American gold assets.
  • Scorpio Gold's Manhattan District project drill results: Trevor Hall reports new results from Scorpio Gold's Phase 2 drill program at the Manhattan District project in Nevada. Hole 109 at the Gold Wedge target delivered "3.38 g per ton gold over 33.31 m from a depth of 138.9 m," including "6.9 g per ton over 12.89 m." Shallower depths within the Zanzibar formation also hit "4 1/2 g per ton over 8.72 m and 2.83 g per ton over 11.89 m."
  • White Gold project preliminary economic assessment: Trevor Hall highlights a positive preliminary economic assessment (PEA) for the White Gold project in Yukon. The PEA outlines an after-tax NPV of "$1.9 billion Canadian," an IRR of "38%," and a "1.7-year payback period" for a "technically straightforward open pit mining operation." The maiden PEA projects a "9.4-year 12,000 ton per day open pit operation" producing an average of "188,000 ounces of gold per year" using a "$3,600 per ounce gold price."
