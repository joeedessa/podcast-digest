Overview

This episode is a no-guest show where Kevin Muir and Patrick Ceresna discuss recent market volatility, focusing on the FOMC meeting, the AI momentum trade's correction, and currency dynamics. Kevin Muir argues that Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish rhetoric without follow-through has created a credibility issue, leading to bond market nervousness and a potential policy error. Patrick Ceresna highlights the extreme short positioning in the Japanese Yen and the potential for a short squeeze, while both hosts express concern about the fragility of the AI-driven equity rally.
