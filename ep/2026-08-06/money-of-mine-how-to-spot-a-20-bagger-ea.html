How to Spot a 20-Bagger Early Rob Cohen

Host: Travis Ricciardo, JD · Guest: Rob Cohen, Gold Fund 1832 Scotiabank

Overview

Rob Cohen, a metallurgist by background and long-time funds manager at 1832 Scotiabank, discusses his career path into funds management and his firm's strategy for identifying early-stage gold opportunities, particularly in Australia and other safe jurisdictions. Cohen emphasizes the importance of geological expertise, a concentrated portfolio, and a willingness to invest on-market rather than being an "index hugger." He shares several past successful investments, highlighting the process of identifying multi-bagger stocks.

Key discussion points

  • Rob Cohen's career path: Cohen started as a mining and mineral process engineer, working for 5 years in the industry at places like BHP Escondida, before pursuing an MBA. He secured an internship at Dynamic Funds after meeting Murray John at Diggers and Dealers in 1996, and has been with the firm ever since.
  • Investment strategy and alpha generation: Cohen's fund is not an "index hugger" and aims to generate alpha by finding opportunities that unit holders cannot do themselves, avoiding large, liquid names like Barrick or Newmont. They focus on concentrated portfolios of 30-35 names.
  • Importance of geological expertise: Cohen, with his metallurgical engineering background, emphasizes that "the rocks you can't fix." His team includes two geologists who provide academic-level analysis, giving the fund "fortitude to invest heavily into fewer names."
  • Focus on Australia: Australia is a key market for Cohen due to its mineral richness and numerous opportunities for new companies, especially in Western Australia, which he believes is "not combed over" like Nevada or Canadian gold camps.
  • Political risk sensitivity: Cohen has become more sensitive to political risks, particularly in the last couple of years, leading him to exit or reduce exposure to regions like Mali, Burkina Faso, Ghana (except for Teranga in Cote d'Ivoire), and Mexico. He now prioritizes "safest jurisdictions as possible."
  • Identifying early-stage winners: Cohen recounts several successful investments, including Northern Star from $1 to over $20, Spartan Resources (formerly Gascoyne) at 40 cents, Founders Metals (10-12 bagger from 40 cents), and Great Bear at $1 a share, which Kinross acquired for $29.
  • Love Auto investment: Cohen identified Love Auto's potential due to its high antimony grade (1.2-1.5%) compared to Perpetua (0.066%). He aggressively bought on-market after China announced export bans on antimony in March 2024, leading to significant returns.
  • Snowline Gold: Cohen views Snowline as the "best undeveloped gold project that belongs in a single asset company," with 7.1 million ounces at 1.34 g/t, a zero strip ratio in its initial open pit phase, and an overall strip ratio of 0.89 to 1.
