Don Durretts Top 12 Gold Silver Stock Picks Why Gold Silver Go Much Higher

Host: Vladyslav Grabarskyy · Guest: Don Durrett, Founder of goldstockdata.com · Listen at source ↗

Overview

Don Durrett argues that central banks are trapped between fighting inflation and stimulating the economy, leading them to accumulate gold. He believes this dynamic, coupled with low gold exposure in the West, sets the stage for significant upside in gold and silver prices, particularly for mining stocks. Durrett emphasizes a speculative approach to mining stocks, targeting multi-bagger returns based on a thesis of much higher gold and silver prices.

Key discussion points

  • Don Durrett states that the Fed's mandate has shifted from full employment and low inflation to prioritizing economic growth, even if it's only 1%.
  • Don Durrett believes the Fed is trapped, facing a crisis regardless of whether they fight inflation (leading to recession) or stimulate the economy (leading to stagflation and more inflation).
  • Don Durrett highlights that central banks and Eastern nations (China, India) are accumulating gold at a rate that exceeds annual mine supply, while the West has minimal gold exposure.
  • Don Durrett uses $7,000 gold and $200 silver as his base case for analyzing gold stocks, looking out two to five years, with a sweet spot of two to three years.
  • Don Durrett expects one more correction in gold, potentially to $3750 before November, before "leg two" of the bull market begins in Q4.
  • Don Durrett projects gold to reach $6500-$7000 in "leg two" within 12 months, followed by a correction, and then a "leg three" to $7500-$8000.
  • Don Durrett identifies "elite eight" gold producers (Agnico, Newmont, Barrick, Lundin Goldfields, Alamos) and "Mormons" (Pan American, Hecla, Coeur, First Majestic, Endeavour Silver, Fresnillo, America's Gold and Silver, Avino) as attractive.
  • Don Durrett prefers producers with growth potential and developers that are in construction or will be in construction by 2028 for their risk-reward profile.
  • Don Durrett avoids royalty companies and exploration plays for big alpha, preferring ETFs for diversification and focusing on producers and developers for speculative returns.
  • Don Durrett mentions "optionality plays" like Southern Silver and Freegold Ventures, where one buys gold/silver in the ground for pennies on the dollar, but notes the uncertainty in potential returns due to board decisions.
  • Don Durrett advises against concentrating investments in a single stock, even if it looks promising, due to the inherent risks in mining.
  • Don Durrett states he will stop buying mining stocks when gold reaches around $5500 and will sell all his mining stocks at the top of "leg three."
