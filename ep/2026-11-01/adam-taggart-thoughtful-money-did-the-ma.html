Did The Market Just Break Lance Roberts

Host: Adam Taggart · Guest: Lance Roberts, Chief Investment Strategist, Real Investment Advice (RIA) · Listen at source ↗

Overview

This episode discusses recent market turbulence, particularly in technology stocks, and the rotation from growth to value. Lance Roberts suggests that while the Magnificent Seven have seen significant weakness, the broader market, especially value stocks, has held up well. He anticipates a potential rotation back into growth stocks as they become oversold, and outlines a strategy for year-end positioning, emphasizing active management and diversification beyond market-cap-weighted indices.

Key discussion points

  • Technology stocks, particularly the Magnificent Seven, have experienced substantial weakness, with $4 trillion in market cap wiped out and four of the seven in bear market territory. Over 60% of S&P 500 stocks are down at least 20% from their highs.
  • Lance Roberts' model flipped from growth to value on October 5th, and value stocks have since outperformed growth stocks. He notes that value is now "extremely overbought" and growth is "extremely oversold," suggesting a potential flip back to growth as early as next week.
  • Despite recent tech earnings, overall earnings expectations for 2027 are still ratcheting higher, with 72% of companies guiding higher, an all-time record. Economic growth in Q3 was 3.2%, driven by imports and business investment, not consumer spending.
  • The market has been trading sideways since June, with the S&P 500 consolidating. A breakdown below the 100-day moving average could lead to a retest of the 200-day moving average around 7,300. A breakout above 7,630 could lead to a year-end rally to 8,000-8,100.
  • AI is discussed as a "game-changer" for productivity, with Adam Taggart using it "agentically" for business tasks. However, concerns are raised about AI's impact on learning for students and the erosion of entry-level job opportunities and internships.
  • The Binance hack highlights security issues in the crypto ecosystem, but Lance Roberts believes it won't alter the perception of crypto enthusiasts. He emphasizes cybersecurity as a necessary holding.
  • Bonds have performed their role as a diversifier during recent equity weakness. Lance Roberts advises caution on chasing bonds currently due to overbought conditions and potential temporary rate increases.
  • The importance of active management and understanding market rotations is highlighted, as passive strategies have yielded little return in the flat market since June, while active strategies can capitalize on swings.
  • Lance Roberts stresses the importance of an Investment Policy Statement (IPS) for investors to build a structured portfolio and avoid reactive decisions based on market sentiment.
