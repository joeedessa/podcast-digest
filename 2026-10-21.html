TL;DR (read this first)

A sharp divide defines today's macro landscape, pitting recession-focused strategists like David Rosenberg against new-paradigm thinkers like Jim Bianco. Rosenberg sees a classic late-cycle setup where Fed tightening will "trip" the economy into a 2027 recession, making long-duration Treasuries and defensive equities the primary safe havens. In contrast, Bianco argues the real risk is that AI succeeds, ushering in a high-growth, high-inflation regime where 5-6% bond yields are the new, appropriate normal. This core disagreement on the future path of growth and inflation is forcing a re-evaluation of all asset classes, from a tech sector widely seen as a bubble to a commodity complex benefiting from geopolitical risk.

  • CENTRAL TENSION: Is the economy heading for a classic recession (Rosenberg) or a high-growth, high-inflation paradigm shift driven by AI (Bianco)?
  • TOP THEME: The AI Bubble Debate: A consensus is forming that tech valuations are stretched, but the reasons for caution diverge sharply.
  • DIVERGENT: BONDS: David Rosenberg is long Treasuries anticipating a recessionary flight to safety, while Jim Bianco is long bonds because current 5-6% yields are appropriate for a new, higher nominal growth regime.
  • HIGH-CONVICTION TRADE: LONG BONDS/TREASURIES, with support from David Rosenberg and Jim Bianco, who both see value despite arriving from different macro theses.
  • CALENDAR: The Anthropic IPO, which Tony Greer flags as a potential "total sell the fact event" for the tech sector.
