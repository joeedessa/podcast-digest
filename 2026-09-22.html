THESIS: The market is deeply divided on whether the AI boom represents a durable technological revolution or a speculative bubble fueled by narrative, with the outcome determining the fate of tech valuations and the broader economy. The core of the debate is whether the massive capital expenditure will generate sustainable returns or prove to be a value-destructive arms race.
