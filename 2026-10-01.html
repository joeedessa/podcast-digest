SPOTLIGHT: The Business Philosophy of a Mining Veteran

Host: Travis Ricciardo, JD Guest: Tony Kiernan, Founding Chair of BC Iron, former Chair of Pilbara Minerals

  • Kiernan's core business philosophy is built on five tenets: control your costs as a price taker, keep a fortress balance sheet, protect the share count, pay dividends, and only do deals that make sense.
  • He was deeply influenced by his father's view: "If you don't pay a dividend, you're not in business."
  • He emphasizes operating in low-risk jurisdictions like Western Australia, having faced challenges with government demands in places like Eritrea.
  • Kiernan believes a board's job is to understand the business and trust the management team, and he is a "great believer in CFOs" as the ultimate source of truth on performance.
  • He is critical of an over-focus on governance at the expense of practical business understanding.

SPOTLIGHT: The Kelly Criterion for Optimal Bet Sizing

Host: Matt Zeigler Guest: Kris Abdelmessih, Moon Tower Zone

  • The Kelly criterion is a formula for optimal bet sizing to maximize long-term compounded growth (CAGR), not to minimize risk.
  • Overbetting Kelly is "an absolute disaster" and is equivalent to "incinerating money," as it lowers the growth rate while rapidly increasing risk.
  • In a study of finance professionals playing a rigged coin-flip game (60% win probability), 28% went bust, demonstrating the common failure to apply proper bet sizing even with a known edge.
  • Abdelmessih's three key rules are: a good bet can be ruined by bad sizing, volatility drag is destructive, and humility in estimating probabilities is crucial because the true odds are rarely known.
