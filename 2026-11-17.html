SPOTLIGHT: Borealis Mining: The Road to Consistent Gold Production

Host: Michael McCrae Guest: Kelly Malcolm, Borealis Mining

  • Borealis Mining is focused on converting its existing inventory of ~2,300 ounces of gold into sales to meet its 2027 guidance of 15,250-19,250 ounces.
  • The company currently has no compliant resources or reserves, which makes providing cost guidance difficult and contributes to its market cap of $115M CAD being below peers.
  • CEO Kelly Malcolm believes demonstrating a minimum of two quarters of actual cash production is the key milestone needed to gain investor trust and achieve a stock re-rating.
  • The Sandman project is pivotal to the company's future, intended to validate its "hub and spoke" model of profiting from assets undesirable to larger miners.
  • An updated resource statement is not expected until late 2027 or early 2028, highlighting the long-term nature of the turnaround story.

SPOTLIGHT: Sierra Madre: Targeting 1500 Tonnes Per Day and Beyond

Host: Trevor Hall Guest: Ken Scott, Sierra Madre Gold and Silver

  • Sierra Madre has successfully completed Phase 1 of its Guitara mine expansion, now operating above its 750 tons per day goal.
  • The company is targeting 1,500 tons per day by Q3 2027 (Phase 2), which will require a $5-6 million investment in a new tailings facility.
  • With $18.5M in cash and $23M in working capital, the company is sufficiently funded for its planned expansion and drill programs.
  • Costs are coming down, beating internal targets for the quarter, and the mine is cash flow positive and profitable at the mine level.
  • The long-term strategy is to build a mid-tier silver producer, targeting 10 million ounces of production, with the Guitara and Del Toro projects expected to contribute 7 million ounces combined by 2028-2029.
