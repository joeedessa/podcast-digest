THESIS: The AI buildout is the market's primary driver, but participants are deeply divided on whether it's a sustainable technological revolution or a speculative bubble vulnerable to rising rates and tightening liquidity. Stacy Rasgon — The Monetary Matters Network argues demand for compute is "off the charts," with hyperscalers projected to spend $1.5 trillion next year, making valuations for leaders like NVDA "absurdly cheap." Ray Dalio — The Julia La Roche Show states that by his indicators, "we are in a bubble," defined by high valuations, significant borrowing to invest, and a gap between created wealth and available money.

KEY TENSION: A significant, sustained downturn in data center capex spending would validate the bears and force a repricing across the entire technology sector.
